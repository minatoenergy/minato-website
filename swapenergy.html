THE CONCEPT

Turn a charging stop into a quick exchange.

Minato Energy's swapping solution: drivers can replace depleted batteries with fully charged ones for a quick and convenient way to keep moving.

  • Designed for high-utilization electric mobility
  • Suitable for battery-swapping station networks
  • Strong fit with e-rickshaw battery applications
  • Built on Minato's battery, electronics and software experience
